Location & Parking — Bright Smile Clinic

Address
Bright Smile Dental Clinic
14 El-Nasr Street, Fifth Settlement, New Cairo, Cairo, Egypt

Landmarks
We are located on the third floor of the Nasr Medical Tower, directly above Cafe Layali and across the street from the Fifth Settlement branch of Seoudi Market. Look for our clinic sign in the building lobby directory.

Getting Here
By car: The clinic is easily accessible from 90th Street and is roughly 10 minutes from the Cairo Festival City area. By ride-hailing app (Uber/Careem): simply search "Bright Smile Dental Clinic, Nasr Medical Tower" — most drivers are familiar with the building. Public transport: microbuses running along 90th Street stop within a 5-minute walk of the building.

Parking
Free parking is available in the basement garage of the Nasr Medical Tower for all clinic patients — simply mention you're visiting Bright Smile Clinic to the garage attendant for a validated ticket. Limited free street parking is also available directly in front of the building, though it fills up quickly during peak hours (weekday evenings). If the basement garage is full, paid parking is available at the Seoudi Market lot across the street.

Accessibility
The building has a working elevator, and our clinic entrance is wheelchair accessible. Please let our staff know in advance if you require any additional accessibility accommodations, and we'll be happy to assist.

For directions or help finding us on the day of your visit, feel free to message us here or call the clinic directly.
