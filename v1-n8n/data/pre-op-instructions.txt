Pre-Operative Instructions — Bright Smile Clinic

Please read the following before your scheduled procedure to help ensure a smooth visit.

General Guidance
Arrive 10 minutes before your appointment time to complete any necessary paperwork. If you are taking blood thinners or have any chronic conditions (diabetes, heart conditions, etc.), please inform our staff at least 48 hours before your appointment.

Before Extractions or Surgical Procedures
Eat a light meal 1-2 hours before your appointment unless instructed otherwise by your doctor. Avoid alcohol for 24 hours prior to the procedure. Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing. Arrange for someone to accompany you if sedation will be used, as you will not be permitted to drive afterward.

Before Root Canal Treatment
Take any prescribed antibiotics exactly as directed leading up to your appointment. Avoid taking pain relievers containing aspirin within 24 hours of your visit, as these can increase bleeding risk. If you are experiencing severe swelling or fever, contact the clinic immediately rather than waiting for your appointment.

Before Whitening or Cosmetic Procedures
Avoid consuming staining foods and drinks (coffee, tea, red wine) for 48 hours before your session for best results.

Medication Disclosure
Please bring a list of all current medications and supplements to your appointment. This helps our doctors plan your treatment safely.

If you have any questions about how to prepare, please contact our front desk — our team is happy to help clarify anything before your visit.
