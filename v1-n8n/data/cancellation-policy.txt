Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy — Bright Smile Clinic

We understand that schedules change. To help us serve all our patients efficiently, please review our cancellation policy below.

Notice Required
We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. This allows us to offer the slot to another patient.

How to Cancel or Reschedule
You can cancel or reschedule by messaging us directly through this chat, calling the clinic during working hours, or visiting us in person. Please note that this assistant can help you book new appointments, but rescheduling and cancellations of existing appointments are currently handled by our staff directly — we'll connect you with them.

Late Cancellations
Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice may be subject to a rescheduling fee of 100 EGP, at the clinic's discretion, particularly for procedures requiring significant chair time (root canals, surgical extractions, crown fittings).

No-Shows
Missing an appointment without any notice may result in a no-show fee of 150 EGP and may affect your ability to book same-day appointments in the future.

Emergency Exceptions
We understand emergencies happen. If you are unable to give notice due to a genuine emergency, please contact us as soon as possible — fees may be waived at the clinic's discretion.

Deposit for Major Procedures
For procedures such as implants, crowns, or orthodontic treatment, a deposit may be required at booking. This deposit is forfeited only in cases of repeated late cancellations or no-shows.

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation in helping us maintain a fair scheduling system for everyone.
