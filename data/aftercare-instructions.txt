Aftercare Instructions — Bright Smile Clinic

Following these guidelines will help your recovery go smoothly and reduce the risk of complications.

After Fillings or Cleanings
You may resume eating once numbness has fully worn off (usually 2-3 hours). Avoid very hot or cold foods for the first 24 hours if sensitivity occurs.

After Extractions
Bite down gently on the gauze provided for 30-45 minutes to help a clot form. Avoid rinsing, spitting forcefully, or using a straw for 24 hours, as this can dislodge the clot and delay healing. Stick to soft, cool foods for the first day. Mild swelling and discomfort are normal for 2-3 days; a cold compress applied in 15-minute intervals can help. Avoid smoking for at least 48 hours, as it significantly slows healing.

After Root Canal Treatment
Some tenderness for a few days is expected. Avoid chewing on the treated tooth until your permanent restoration is placed. Continue brushing and flossing normally, being gentle around the treated area.

After Crown or Bridge Placement
Avoid sticky or hard foods for the first 24 hours while the cement fully sets.

When to Contact Us Immediately
If you experience severe or worsening pain, heavy bleeding that doesn't stop after 30 minutes of pressure, significant facial swelling, fever, or difficulty breathing or swallowing, please contact the clinic right away or seek emergency care — do not wait for your next scheduled appointment.

General Oral Hygiene
Continue brushing twice daily and flossing once daily unless told otherwise for a specific procedure. Attend your follow-up appointment as scheduled to ensure proper healing.
