Payment Methods — Bright Smile Clinic

We offer several convenient ways to pay for your treatment.

Accepted Payment Methods
- Cash (EGP)
- Visa and Mastercard (credit and debit)
- Mobile wallets: Vodafone Cash, InstaPay, and Fawry
- Bank transfer (for larger procedures such as implants or orthodontic treatment, available on request)

Installment Plans
For major procedures such as dental implants, crowns, bridges, or orthodontic treatment, we offer installment plans over 3 to 6 months through select partner banks. Please ask our staff for current eligibility and terms, as these may vary depending on the procedure and total cost.

Insurance Coverage
We work directly with several insurance providers (see our Insurance Coverage information for details). If your provider is on our accepted list, please bring your insurance card and any required pre-authorization documents to your appointment. Coverage percentages vary by provider and procedure — our staff can help you understand your specific coverage before treatment begins.

Deposits
A deposit may be required at the time of booking for major procedures (implants, crowns, orthodontics) to secure your appointment slot. This amount is deducted from your final treatment cost.

Receipts and Invoices
An itemized receipt is provided after every visit. If you require a formal invoice for insurance reimbursement or personal records, please let our front desk know and we'll prepare one for you.

Questions About Pricing
For a full price list of our procedures, feel free to ask this assistant directly, or speak with our front desk team for a personalized cost estimate based on your specific treatment plan.
